# April 2026 Support Plan For Jay And Nupura

## Purpose

This note defines where Jay and Nupura can most usefully support April execution for Mitozz Japan.

The goal is not to split core creative ownership in too many directions.
The goal is to help the current growth engine run more consistently by supporting:

- publishing discipline
- organic Instagram follow-through
- audience-signal gathering
- faster approvals and decisions
- trust-building around the brand

## Current Situation

Mitozz already has the main April organic Instagram system in motion:

- `3` main feed posts per week
- at least `1` Reel per week
- same-day support Stories on feed days
- one extra interactive Story lane when useful

The account is still early.
The latest metrics snapshot from `2026-03-31` shows:

- discovery is starting
- most views are from non-followers
- Reels are doing most of the reach work
- follower growth and engagement are still very early

That means April should focus on making the system more consistent and more responsive, not adding random new workstreams.

## Strategic Call

The best use of Jay and Nupura in April is to support the organic Instagram system around execution, not to take over the creative-production core.

That means:

- Ernie stays closest to strategy, creative direction, and asset-production logic
- Jay helps remove decision friction and adds business/trust inputs
- Nupura helps keep the publishing, Story, and feedback loop active

If we do this well, Mitozz should get:

- more posting consistency
- better Story support around each feed post
- clearer audience questions and objections
- faster movement from post -> interaction -> next topic

## Priority Areas Where They Can Help

### 1. Organic Instagram Publishing Support

This is the highest-leverage support lane.

What needs to happen:

- make sure approved posts and Stories actually go out on schedule
- make sure the correct caption, hashtags, and asset links are used
- make sure Stories are not skipped on feed days
- make sure polls and question boxes are posted when planned

Why it matters:

- the April strategy depends on consistency more than complexity
- missed Story support weakens the launch-phase growth system
- the account needs repeated touchpoints before early reach becomes trust and follows

### 2. Audience Signal Collection

This is the second priority.

What needs to happen:

- collect replies, poll results, question-box answers, comments, and DMs
- turn recurring questions into future content ideas
- flag which posts seem to generate more curiosity, saves, shares, or profile visits

Why it matters:

- April content is educational and trust-led
- the fastest way to improve it is to learn what people are actually asking
- Story interaction is not mainly for vanity numbers right now; it is for signal collection

### 3. Trust And Proof Support

This is especially important for Mitozz's premium positioning.

What needs to happen:

- identify useful proof points, FAQs, expert-aligned talking points, and reassurance themes
- surface recurring buyer concerns like safety, quality, ingredients, and brand credibility
- help gather ideas for future FAQ, trust, and highlight content

Why it matters:

- April has several trust-focused posts
- premium wellness growth depends on reassurance and explanation, not hype
- good trust inputs make both Stories and feed posts stronger

### 4. Faster Decision-Making

What needs to happen:

- give fast approvals when something is ready
- avoid long feedback loops on minor points
- escalate only when there is a real strategic or compliance concern

Why it matters:

- the current system already has strong strategic direction
- execution slows down most when decisions sit too long
- fast clean decisions protect cadence

- strategic clarity
- fast decision-maker

Nupura should be the operational owner for:

- posting consistency
- Story follow-through
- audience-signal capture

Jay and Nupura can share these tasks:

- flagging which content feels strongest or weakest
- helping decide which trust or FAQ angles need reinforcement next

- Jay = direction and judgment

## Organic Instagram Operating Plan For April

### Main Objective

Keep the launch-phase Instagram engine consistent:

- Reels for reach
- carousels for saves and trust
- Stories for reinforcement, interaction, and account activity

### What Support Is Most Needed On Instagram

1. Make sure every planned feed post is paired with its support Story on the same day.
2. Do not let interactive Story days slip, because that is where we gather useful audience signals.
3. After each post, capture what people respond to so the next week's content gets smarter.
4. Keep the account feeling active and intentional, even though it is still early-stage.

### What They Should Start Doing Immediately

### Jay Immediate Actions

- approve high-level April priorities and trust angles
- answer open business or product-context questions quickly
- flag any sensitive claims or wording that should be handled carefully
- surface the top `3-5` customer concerns that content should keep addressing

### Nupura Immediate Actions

- own the weekly posting checklist
- post approved Stories on time, especially same-day support Stories
- add polls/question boxes where already planned
- log comments, replies, and questions in one simple running note
- send a short weekly feedback summary: what people engaged with, what questions appeared, what seems unclear

## Suggested Weekly Rhythm

### Monday

- confirm this week's post dates
- confirm which Stories need native interaction stickers
- flag anything still waiting for approval

### Midweek

- check whether the first post and Story support went out cleanly
- note any replies, comments, or questions
- escalate only if there is a real issue or repeated confusion

### Friday

- review what was published that week
- summarize top audience signals
- decide whether next week's Stories or captions should respond to anything learned

- too many standalone Story days
- trend-chasing content that does not fit the brand
- major rewrites of approved creative direction
- slow multi-person feedback loops on small details
- new channels or side projects that distract from Instagram consistency

## Success Markers For April

If this support plan is working, we should see:

- fewer missed or delayed posts
- stronger same-day Story support
- more useful questions, poll responses, and audience signals
- clearer understanding of what themes create curiosity and trust
- a smoother operating rhythm around the April calendar

## Bottom Line

The highest-value support from Jay and Nupura in April is not extra creative ideation.

It is helping the current Mitozz system run properly:

- Jay supports direction, trust, and fast decisions
